What UVH does

UVH lets you create and manage short links, change their destinations, apply redirection rules, connect custom domains, protect links, view analytics, collaborate in workspaces and automate operations using API tokens and webhooks.

A redirection may depend on the link's status, schedule, click limit, single-use setting, password or configured rules. The primary destination acts as a fallback when no rule matches. Some features require a verified account, a particular role or MFA.

The Service does not host or review destination website content in advance, buy domains on your behalf or guarantee that an external destination is available.

The short address is an identifier within the service, rather than a domain purchase or a guarantee that an alias will be retained forever. You can change a destination without changing the link, subject to the resource's status, permissions and rules. Check the result after changing destinations, rules or campaign parameters, particularly before distributing a link in communications or printed materials.

Measurement shows events admitted by the system. Counts may depend on the availability of analytics logging, security blocks and link rules. They are not a certified audit of unique individuals or proof that someone has bought, read or accepted anything at the destination.
